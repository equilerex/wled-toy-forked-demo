Re-encoded from the 256 kbps download to 128 kbps stereo MP3 to keep the bundle small:

    ffmpeg -i sigmamusicart-no-copyright-music-537751.mp3 -map 0:a -map_metadata -1 -codec:a libmp3lame -b:a 128k audio.mp3
